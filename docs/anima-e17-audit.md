## 4. Temporal causality (consistent / inconsistent / unresolved)

- REV1 alignment A is measured from the FIRST REV presentation's own
  spike activity, generated by PRE-REV1 weights (T0 snapshots; the
  presentation starts at the same instant the weights are at their
  T0 state) -> re-expression is **temporally consistent** as the
  initiator; no structural event precedes the flip within the
  presentation except the presentation itself.
- M3 maturations occur 200-300 ms INTO the window (t=365200-365300 /
  369300) — after the opening spike quartiles; their combined weight
  (0.06-0.12) cannot carry a 0.5-cosine change -> **temporally
  consistent with a modifier, insufficient as initiator**.
- M4 prunes: 20260912 none during; 9001/424242 4-5 during,
  removing near-weightless synapses; no side-selective removal ->
  **temporally consistent with maintenance, not initiation**.
- M6: continuous, endpoint net-negative across all seeds; no
  excitatory direct path; **activity-mediated; quantitative role
  unresolved**.
- M2: re-pins every 100 ms; invariant holds; side-neutral;
  **consistent with maintaining competition** (already shown NOT
  necessary for the reversal — E16 M2-off).
- Unresolved: sub-presentation flip timing (no measurement exists at
  finer than presentation resolution); M6's contribution magnitude.

No causality claimed from precedence; the artifact-supported
ordering is: T0 asymmetric/balanced weights + reversed input
statistics -> first-presentation re-expression -> concurrent small
M3/M4/M6 modifications.

## 5. Passive re-expression test — SUPPORTED (no contradiction found)

1. With STDP emitting zero events and topology changing by <1.5% of
   total weight, the REV1 cosine flip (0.47-0.62) occurs in all
   seeds — the only event-scale change is the phase-order reversal
   (8-11 -> 4-7 vs 4-7 -> 8-11).
2. Both A-side (13.4-14.3) and C-side (14.5-15.1) afferent weight
   pools exist at T0: the readout machinery for both alignments is
   present before the flip.
3. M2 demonstrably maintains the competitive regime (invariant
   everywhere); M3/M6 demonstrably modify the trajectory afterwards
   (maturations during; inhibitory drift) — matching the hypothesis
   roles. No artifact contradicts the re-expression account.

## 6. Candidate reduction (evidence, direction, status)

- **Re-expression (activity statistics over pre-existing weights):
  primary candidate.** Operates; temporally consistent; magnitude-
  consistent; direction consistent (E9 trailing-side law: trailing
  4-7 at REV1 drives the A-side readout through pre-existing 4-7
  afferents). Not ruled out by any existing ablation (STDP-off AND
  M2-off both still flip — consistent with re-expression surviving
  each).
- **M3: modifier candidate.** Operates (maturations during the
  window, all seeds); direction mixed (20260912 3x upper-B vs
  9001 all-lower-B vs 424242 2/3 lower-B) while the flip is uniform
  -> side-inconsistent as initiator; magnitude-inconsistent
  (<0.3% weight). Not yet ablated in the STDP-free regime: a new
  ablation can distinguish its role there.
- **M4: maintenance candidate.** Operates (mostly outside the
  window); removes near-zero-weight synapses; magnitude- and
  timing-inconsistent as initiator. Ablatable only jointly with M3
  (shared flag disable_m3_m4).
- **M6: activity-modulator candidate.** Operates (87%/79%/87% of
  inhibitory synapses reweighted, net -1.0..-1.4); no excitatory
  direct path; necessity untestable under the frozen gates (v2-M6
  rate-failed at 301.7 Hz). Not ruled out, not required by any
  evidence.
- **M2, STDP: not necessary** for the reversal (E16, 9/9).
- **M5: inactive.**

The evidence makes re-expression the single strongest candidate and
identifies exactly one untested structural trajectory in the
STDP-free regime (M3+M4 topology dynamics). One ablation against the
committed STDP-off control:

- **Control**: committed STDP-off arms (E16 stdpoff; NEVER rerun).
- **Ablation**: STDP-off + **M3+M4-off** (`disable_m3_m4 = true` on
  the e16-stdpoff base; existing flag, no code change, no new

- **Frozen substrate**: exact E12 curriculum (60 SEQ / 60 REV,
  variant_block 60), E6 on, M2 on, M6 on, adaptation on, seed
  20260912 only (cross-seed gated on gates + informative outcome).
- **Primary endpoint**: E14 sustained k* rule + T0/REV1/REV10/REV60
  trajectory + frozen gates (0 failures, rates [20,250],
  engagement).
- **Counterfactuals**: if re-expression alone suffices -> k* = 1
  reproduces (flip at REV1 with ZERO topology dynamics, ZERO STDP);
  if M3/M4 topology is necessary in the STDP-free regime -> no
  re-anchoring (alignment C sustained, k* fails by REV60).
- **Why it distinguishes**: the arm differs from the committed
  stdpoff control in exactly one way — absence of topology dynamics;
  everything else (input statistics, weights, M2, M6, decay) is
  identical. Flip survival vs failure cleanly separates
  "re-expression alone" from "structural initiation required".

## 8. Scientific limits

ESTABLISHED: E16 non-necessity of M2 and STDP individually (9/9
k*=1); M2 required for the canonical separated regime; canonical
reproduction. E17 audit: in STDP-off, the flip occurs with zero STDP
events and <1.5% topology weight change; pre-existing A/C weight
pools near-balanced at T0; M3 maturations all inside the window on
mixed sides; recurrent structure collapsed (~1 synapse); M6 broad
net-negative inhibitory movement; M5 inactive.

TEMPORALLY/SPATIALLY CONSISTENT (not causal): re-expression as
initiator; M3/M4 as modifiers; M2 as regime maintainer.

UNRESOLVED: M6's quantitative contribution (no viable arm under the
gates); sub-presentation flip timing; the magnitude share of silent
weight movement within the interval; M3's role in the STDP-ON
organism.

WHAT THE PROPOSED EXPERIMENT WOULD ESTABLISH: necessity of M3+M4
topology dynamics for the flip in the STDP-free regime (per the
frozen decision rule), i.e., whether re-expression alone suffices.

WHAT IT WOULD NOT ESTABLISH: sufficiency; M6 necessity (gate-
blocked); the flip's mechanism under STDP-ON; generality beyond
seed 20260912 at this scale; any causal claim for any mechanism not
demonstrated by an ablation under the frozen decision rule.

No mechanism is called causal before an ablation demonstrates
