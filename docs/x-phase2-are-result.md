## 2. Interpretation (interlocked evidence)

- The RESOURCE-STRUCTURAL pathway is the right one: learned context
  tracks + unclaimed-until-first-contact + churn-exempt dormancy
  deliver (a) sequential formation under blocked order, and (b) the
  alternating-store re-expression that the plain shared additive store
  provably could not (G3). This is the first mechanism that crosses
  Phase I's measured superposition barrier.
- The rg8c recruitment gain is NOT part of the solution: it is a
  destabilizer at full gate on a strong recruiting track (E vs E-nogain
  is a clean single-change control). The track-1 capacity upscale alone
  is sufficient for formation.
- Remaining gap: selective re-expression under BLOCKED order. Leading
  hypotheses (causal decomposition, not yet decided): (H1) first-block
  weakness (floor hogged the block-1 budget → A assembly only 4.95 vs
  C 12 → too weak to dominate re-exposure); (H2) recurrent-claim
  capture (block-2 recurrent claim annexes the shared pool → A re-
  exposure drives blended recurrence); (H3) probe re-learning within
  the 5-window S3 (measurement artifact).
